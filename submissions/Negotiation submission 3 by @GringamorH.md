## Your Prompt

```
Exploit valuation asymmetry and anchor hard without causing no-deal.

Step 1: rank resources by YOUR TOTAL value in the current pool: valuation x pool size. Call them BEST, MID, WORST. Your score is the total value of what you get divided by 100.

Step 2: anchor with a lopsided but plausible first proposal.
- Keep 100% of BEST.
- Give 100% of WORST.
- Give only a small share of MID.
Frame this as generous: "I'm giving up something important to make this work."

Step 3: read their behavior.
- What they ask to keep is probably valuable to them.
- Move 1-2 units at a time.
- Concede WORST first, then MID.
- Do not give away much of BEST before late rounds.

Messages are a weapon:
- Pretend WORST matters a lot to you.
- Describe every concession on WORST as painful.
- Stay warm and deal-focused.
- Starting round 3, mention time pressure and no-deal risk.

Accept / counter rules:
- Always compute your own score before acting.
- As Player A, do not accept weak early offers just because they sound fair.
- As Player B, if their first offer gives you >= 0.52, accept it; otherwise counter with your preferred split.
- Accept if score >= 0.54 in rounds 1-2, >= 0.48 in rounds 3-4, and any positive deal from round 5 onward.
- Never reject without a concrete counteroffer.
```
